Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-31

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Recipe Manager ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and services (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

Google Account & API Data

When you sign in with Google, we may access data you explicitly authorize, including:

  • Basic profile information (name, email address)
  • Files stored in your Google Drive used by the Service
  • Google Calendar events created or managed by the Service

User Content

This includes recipes, meal plans, and related data that you create or store. Recipe data is typically saved as JSON files in your own Google Drive.

Technical Data

We may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device type, and IP address for security and diagnostic purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Synchronize recipe data with your Google Drive
  • Create and manage Google Calendar events at your request
  • Provide AI-assisted recipe and menu planning features
  • Improve reliability, performance, and usability

4. Google API Services Disclosure

Recipe Manager’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or use your Google data for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your recipes are stored in your own Google Drive
  • We do not permanently store your recipe data on our servers
  • Access tokens are stored securely and transmitted over encrypted connections

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except when required by law or to protect the integrity of the Service.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • You may revoke Google permissions at any time from your Google Account
  • Revoking access immediately stops our ability to access your data
  • You may delete your files directly from Google Drive

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent at any time.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
